Privacy Policy

Wordkit · Last updated 27 November 2026

Wordkit does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it makes no network requests.

The text you type or open, your favorites and your settings are saved only on your Mac and are never sent anywhere. You can turn off keeping your text in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything.
